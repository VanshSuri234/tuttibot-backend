## 🔧 Customization

To modify visualizations, edit `visualize_results.py`:

```python
# Change time range for timeline (Plot 08)
time_limit = 15.0  # Show first 15 seconds instead of 10

# Change error thresholds (Plot 03)
thresholds = [20, 40, 80, 160, 320]  # Use different thresholds

# Change colors
color_map = {
    'staccato': '#ff0000',    # Pure red
    'legato': '#0000ff',      # Pure blue
    'uncertain': '#808080'    # Gray
}
```

## 📊 Interpretation Guidelines

### Good Signs ✅:
- **Plot 01**: Blue line tracks green line closely
- **Plot 02**: Median << Mean (few outliers)
- **Plot 03**: >80% within 200ms
- **Plot 04**: Low "uncertain" percentage (<30%)
- **Plot 06**: Most dots below 200ms line
- **Plot 07**: Small boxes (low IQR)
- **Plot 08**: Clear color separation by duration
- **Plot 10**: Staccato duration < Legato duration

### Warning Signs ⚠️:
- **Plot 01**: Major deviations from linear
- **Plot 02**: Very wide distribution
- **Plot 03**: <60% within 200ms
- **Plot 04**: >50% uncertain
- **Plot 06**: Many dots above 500ms
- **Plot 07**: Large boxes or many outliers
- **Plot 10**: No duration difference between articulations
